# Add DCnet Buddy tab

Adds DCnet Buddy with activity cards, covers, status badges, search and active-game/library filters. Shared service entries count once in totals while titles keep their own library matches. PBA Bowling 2001 and Sega Rally 2 use direct status probes when the feed omits them; those probes report responsiveness without player or match counts. Owned titles can launch from their details, with a copy selector when multiple files match.

## Find an online game and launch your copy

1. Open **DCnet** in a standalone build with DCnet Buddy and an internet connection. Wait for the dashboard to show its activity update.
2. Search for a game or player. Use **Active games only** to narrow the results to active games, or **Owned games only** to show games matched to your Hollycast library.
3. Open a game's details to see the available status. For a matched title, launch from the details and choose a copy if Hollycast offers several.
