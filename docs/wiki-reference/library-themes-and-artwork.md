# Add Pocket Dimension, Demo Disc and Warning! with shared artwork support

Pocket Dimension, Demo Disc, and Warning! give the library different layouts. They share the artwork settings for TheGamesDB, ScreenScraper, and LaunchBox. TheGamesDB is the default and can supply artwork missing from your chosen provider.
