# Use a phone as a VMU

On standalone Windows, macOS, or Linux, open **Settings → On-screen display → Phone VMU** and enable **Connect Phone**. Put the phone and host on the same local network. Scan the QR code or open the displayed address, then choose a VMU slot. The browser can show its LCD or play a minigame. Use [Phone VMU save-back](phone-vmu-save-back.md) for returning minigame progress to Hollycast.

## Use the phone display or play a VMU game

1. In standalone Hollycast on Windows, macOS or Linux, connect your phone and PC to the same local network.
2. Open **Settings → On-screen display → Phone VMU** and turn on **Connect Phone**.
3. Scan the QR code or open the displayed address. Pick the slot that contains the card you want.
4. To play a mini-game stored on that card, select **Play mini-game on this card**. Use the browser's VMU controls and follow the [save-back steps](phone-vmu-save-back.md) when you finish.

## Mate two adult Chao and bring the eggs back

Use a build with experimental **Create VMU link** and **Join VMU link** controls.

1. Use the Chao Transporter in *Sonic Adventure* to put one adult Chao on each of two VMUs, such as A1 and A2.
2. Open Phone VMU on the PC, select the first card and choose **Play mini-game on this card**. Press **A+B** to start *Chao Adventure*, choose **Mating**, and stop at **Please Connect**.
3. On the PC, choose **Controls → Create VMU link**. Open the invite on your phone, choose the other slot, and start its mini-game. On the phone, reach **Please Connect**, then tap **Join VMU link**.
4. Wait for **Please Disconnect** on both screens. Choose **Disconnect VMU link** once, then let both animations finish. On each VMU, press **A**, press **Mode**, and choose **Return to Dreamcast**. Keep both pages open until the saves finish syncing.
5. With your character empty-handed, jump onto the Transporter's red button. Select the first VMU's egg, then repeat with the second VMU. Choose the egg alone to leave its parent on the card.

Each VMU gets its own egg. You can bring both eggs into the same garden.
